I'm **Himashree Kalita** — a product manager with 7 years across fintech, consumer internet, and growth, 5 of them in product. I currently lead product for the Mutual Funds pod at **smallcase**, where I led the build of a SEBI-regulated investment platform from zero to one.

I'm drawn to two kinds of hard problems, and I've spent my career bouncing between them on purpose:

- **Regulated 0-to-1** — building products inside real constraints (SEBI, KYC, payment rails, settlement) where correctness is non-negotiable and the reward is owning the whole experience and its economics.
- **Consumer growth & monetization** — moving acquisition, retention, and revenue curves for millions of users, where the reward is finding the lever everyone assumed was already pulled.

The thread between them is the same: I like understanding a system deeply enough to find the assumption everyone treats as fixed — and then deleting it. A mandatory CAN. A subscription-only pricing model. The idea that "these users won't pay."

## How I work

- **Instrumentation before opinions.** I'd rather find the real drop-off than argue about the imagined one.
- **Technical enough to go deep.** B.Tech in CS; comfortable in SQL, MongoDB, and reading the system, so I can have the real conversation with engineering instead of a translated one.
- **Outcome-owned, not output-owned.** I measure myself on the metric that moved, and I'll say "not yet" until the money flows are right.
- **Cross-functional by default.** Most of my wins involved aligning engineering, data, ops, CX, marketing, and partnerships around one number.
