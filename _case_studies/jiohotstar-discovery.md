> **TL;DR** — On one of India's largest streaming platforms, I ran two distinct discovery bets: a **cohort-based recommendation strategy** that lifted **home-page CTR by ~10%**, and a **"Trending Now" tray** that grew **mid-tier content views by 18%** — while reducing manual curation load on a 20-person publishing team.

## Context

At streaming scale, discovery *is* the product — what surfaces on the home rails decides what gets watched. JioHotstar leaned heavily on a large editorial team to hand-curate, which is high-effort, hard to scale, and biased toward already-popular ("head") content. Personalization was shallow, and strong mid-tier content stayed buried.

## Problem

- Recommendations weren't personalized — every viewer saw largely the same editorial grid regardless of what they'd been watching.
- **Editorial curation didn't scale** and concentrated attention on a thin slice of head content.
- A deep catalog of **mid-tier content was under-discovered**, leaving engagement (and the value of that catalog) on the table.

## What I did

**1. Cohort-based recommendation strategy** *(→ home-page CTR +10%)*
- Bifurcated users into **cohorts based on their recent watch history**, then served each cohort a **different set of content** on the home page.
- Made each cohort's home page a **blend of manually published trays/rails and machine-driven ones** — editorial judgment where it mattered, algorithmic reach everywhere else.
- Net result: a **~10% lift in overall home-page CTR.**

**2. "Trending Now" tray** *(→ mid-tier content views +18%)*
- Launched a **dynamic, behavior-driven tray** that surfaced real-time momentum rather than waiting on manual curation.
- It pulled attention toward titles beyond the usual head content, **growing mid-tier content views by 18%.**

**Underneath both**
- Partnered with **ML teams to refine ranking logic**, increasing the share of machine-curated content and tuning it toward quality mid-tier titles, not just hits.
- **Reduced editorial overhead** for the ~20-person publishing team by letting the system carry more of the curation load — freeing them for higher-value editorial calls.

## Impact

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Home-page CTR (cohort recommendations) | **+10%** |
| Mid-tier content views ("Trending Now") | **+18%** |
| Machine-curated share of surface | **Increased** |
| Editorial workload (20-person team) | **Reduced** |

## What I learned

- At scale, the PM's job in discovery is **designing the system that makes the decisions**, not making the decisions — and the best system blends editorial judgment with algorithmic reach rather than choosing one.
- "Surface more of the head" is the easy local optimum; the real value was **rescuing the long/mid tail** — which needed a dedicated surface (Trending Now) and an explicit objective, or the model just amplifies what's already winning.
- Automating part of a human team's job works when you **reframe it as freeing them for higher-value work**, which is also how you get their buy-in.
