> **TL;DR** — On one of India's largest streaming platforms, I shifted content discovery from editorial curation toward cohort-based, machine-curated recommendations — launching "Trending Now" and lifting **CTR +10%** and **mid-tier content views +18%**, while reducing manual workload for a 20-person publishing team.

## Context

At streaming scale, discovery *is* the product — what surfaces on the home rails decides what gets watched. JioHotstar leaned heavily on a large editorial team to hand-curate, which is high-effort, hard to scale, and biased toward already-popular ("head") content. Strong mid-tier content stayed buried.

## Problem

- **Editorial curation didn't scale** and concentrated attention on a thin slice of head content.
- A deep catalog of **mid-tier content was under-discovered**, leaving engagement (and the value of that catalog) on the table.
- Recommendations weren't sufficiently personalized to viewing cohorts.

## What I did

- **Introduced a cohort-based recommendation strategy** — segmenting viewers and tailoring what surfaced to each cohort instead of a one-size-fits-all editorial grid.
- **Launched "Trending Now"** — a dynamic, behavior-driven rail that captured real-time momentum rather than waiting on manual curation.
- **Partnered with ML teams to refine ranking logic**, increasing the share of machine-curated content on the surface and tuning it to push quality mid-tier titles, not just hits.
- **Reduced editorial overhead** for the ~20-person publishing team by letting the system carry more of the curation load — freeing them for higher-value editorial calls.

## Impact

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Click-through rate | **+10%** |
| Mid-tier content views | **+18%** |
| Machine-curated share of surface | **Increased** |
| Editorial workload (20-person team) | **Reduced** |

## What I learned

- At scale, the PM's job in discovery is **designing the system that makes the decisions**, not making the decisions. The leverage is in the ranking objective and the human-in-the-loop balance.
- "Surface more of the head" is the easy local optimum; the real value was **rescuing the long/mid tail** — which needed an explicit objective, or the model just amplifies what's already winning.
- Automating part of a human team's job works when you **reframe it as freeing them for higher-value work**, which is also how you get their buy-in.
